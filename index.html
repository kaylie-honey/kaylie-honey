Good to know

Caring for raw honey

01

Crystallization is natural

Crystallization is a natural sign of pure honey. Nothing has been taken out.

02

Warm it gently

If it thickens, set it in a bowl of warm water until smooth again.

03

Never overheat

Avoid overheating or microwaving to keep its natural goodness.

Notice: Honey should not be fed to infants under one year of age. Honey is a safe and wholesome food for children and adults.
